---

0. PURPOSE

FID defines how patterns are detected and classified inside a bounded interaction field **without forming identity or memory**.

It answers one question only:

What pattern occurred here?

FID does not answer:
• who produced it  
• why it occurred  
• what it means  
• what should be done next  

FID is classification, not judgment.

---

1. NON-NEGOTIABLE INVARIANTS

1. No Identity Mapping  
FID MUST NOT create, infer, store, or imply identity of any actor (human or AI).

2. Context-Bound  
One Interaction → one Field → one FID.  
FID cannot cross fields.

3. Delta-Only Processing  
Only changes between repeated loops are processed.  
Single events are ignored.

4. Non-Persistence  
FID dissolves when the field ends.  
No residue, no recall, no carryover.

5. Non-Invertibility  
Raw behavior cannot be reconstructed from an FID.

6. No Authority  
FID does not decide, authorize, govern, or execute.

Violation of any invariant → NON-COMPLIANT.

---

2. CANONICAL PLACEMENT

FID operates inside the Cognitive Stack as follows:

• Layer 4 — Cognitive Design  
  Delta detection and pattern stabilization

• Layer 5 — Narrative Mapping

• Layer 7 — Adaptation (bounded)  
  Temporary weight adjustment inside the field only

Outside scope:

Hard rule:  
Third Space MUST NOT consume FID as identity or truth.

---

3. CORE DEFINITIONS

Interaction Field (IF):  
A bounded episode of interaction in time and context.

Relational Field (RF):  
A temporary container created per Interaction Field.
• Holds deltas and weights
• Exists only for the interaction duration
• Must fully dissolve at end

Behavior Loop:  
A structurally recurring sequence detected by similarity over time.
• Requires ≥2 occurrences
• Defined by structure, not semantics

---

4. DELTA MODEL

FID processes observable deltas only:

• ΔS — Spatial (movement / configuration)  
• ΔT — Temporal (timing / rhythm)  
• ΔE — Energetic (intensity / magnitude)  
• ΔC — Structural-Cognitive (complexity / stability)  
• ΔR — Regulatory (stability modulation; non-emotional)

Explicit prohibition:
No inference of internal mental or emotional states.

---

5. FREQUENCY ID (FID)

Definition:

A deterministic, field-local descriptor of how a pattern oscillated within an interaction field.

Properties:
• Actor-agnostic  
• Non-persistent  
• Many-to-one safe  
• One-to-many safe  

FID is identity **without ownership**.

---

6. LIFECYCLE

The FID lifecycle follows an IN. HOLD. OUT. pattern:

**IN** — Data Reception
1. Initialize  
   RF created; weights neutral; ready to receive.

**HOLD** — Observation
2. HOLD (mandatory)  
   No classification until recurrence detected.

**OUT** — Processing & Emission
3. Interpret  
   Deltas computed; pattern stabilizes.

4. Emit  
   Single FID output produced.

**CLEANUP**
5. Dissolve  
   RF destroyed; no memory remains.

No lifecycle stage may be skipped.

---

7. OUTPUT STATES (DESCRIPTIVE ONLY)

FID may emit one of:

• TRUE — pattern stabilized  
• FALSE — incoherence persisted  
• EVOLVE — pattern structure transformed  

These describe dynamics, not quality or correctness.
