# THE META FRAME v2  
**Presence · Decision · Event · Value**

This document supersedes THE_META_FRAME.md.
It does not invalidate it.
It formalizes what emerged after the introduction of the Third Space.

---

## 0. Purpose

This document defines the **non-negotiable structural frame** of a system where:

- truth is preserved,
- decisions are explicit,
- irreversible transitions are governed,
- and value is allowed to materialize **without corrupting reality**.

It is **not** a product specification.  
It is **not** an implementation guide.  
It is **not** a commercial promise.

It is a **constitutional layer**.

Everything built beneath this frame must comply with it.  
Nothing below it may rewrite it.

This document is intended to be **public**, **referenceable**, and **unownable**.

---

## On Naming (Important Clarification)

The names **UPAD**, **ISPAD**, and **KAPIA** are used in this document as **conceptual labels** for distinct regimes of interaction and responsibility.

They describe **moral and structural domains**, not guaranteed products, feature sets, or commercial commitments.

At different times, these regimes may correspond to products, services, systems, or internal tools—but this document does **not** define their implementation, roadmap, or monetization.

The names exist here to preserve clarity of structure, not to bind future execution.

## 1. First Principle

**Truth precedes value.**  
Value never precedes truth.

If something happened, it can be registered.  
If it did not happen, nothing else matters.

Truth is not negotiated.  
Truth is not interpreted post-hoc.  
Truth is not monetized.

---

## 2. Foundational Primitives

### 2.1 Presence

Presence is the atomic unit of truth.

Presence is:
- verifiable,
- time-bound,
- contextual,
- append-only,
- non-revocable.

Presence is **not** identity.  
Presence is **not** reputation.  
Presence is **not** value.

Presence is **fact**.

---

### 2.2 Event

An Event is a **bounded reality** composed of:
- multiple presences,
- a temporal window,
- an operational context,
- and a holding authority.

An Event is not:
- a marketing activity,
- a ticket bundle,
- a set of media artifacts.

An Event becomes a **first-class asset** only when:
- presence is verified,
- coherence is held,
- and the event is explicitly finalized.

---

### 2.3 Continuity

Continuity binds presences and events across time.

Continuity ensures:
- history cannot be rewritten,
- truth compounds instead of dissolves,
- future actions can reference past reality without reinterpretation.

Continuity is law.

## 3. Regimes of Reality (Non-Collapsible)

### 3.1 UPAD — Inbound / Attendee Regime

UPAD represents the **market and experience regime**.

Its truth is the **human experience**.

It is allowed to be:
- reversible,
- commercial,
- persuasive,
- optimized.

UPAD captures demand and revenue.  
UPAD must never decide truth.

---

### 3.2 ISPAD — Operator / Governance Regime

ISPAD represents the **coordination and accountability regime**.

Its truth is the **event’s operation**.

It is allowed to:
- plan,
- allocate responsibility,
- prepare settlements,
- coordinate stakeholders.

ISPAD prepares consequences.  
ISPAD must never finalize reality.

---

### 3.3 KAPIA — Gate / Sovereignty Regime

KAPIA represents the **sovereign boundary regime**.

Its truth is **irreversible reality**.

It decides only:
- valid / invalid,
- in / out,
- happened / did not happen.

KAPIA does not explain itself.  
KAPIA does not monetize.  
KAPIA does not negotiate.

---

## 4. The Third Space (Decision Kernel)

The Third Space is the **deterministic interface** between:
- human decision-making,
- and system execution.

It exists to:
- isolate agreement,
- stabilize truth,
- prevent premature or ambiguous execution.

### 4.1 State Law

All decisions pass through:

**HOLD → DECIDE → COMMIT**

- **HOLD**: reality acknowledged, no action allowed.  
- **DECIDE**: a human authority explicitly accepts responsibility.  
- **COMMIT**: the decision becomes irreversible and executable.

A decision is resolved **once**, logged, and reused.

---

### 4.2 Dual Validation (Human–AI Co-Agency)

- **AI validator**  
  Guards structure, invariants, and lawful transitions.  
  AI never owns authority.

- **Human governor**  
  Owns meaning, consequence, and irreversibility.

Machines constrain process.  
Humans own reality.

Their intersection produces a **governed decision artifact**.

---

## 5. Live Event State

During a live event, the system may enter a **temporary high-integrity mode**.

In Live Event State:
- time sensitivity increases,
- interpretation is suspended,
- truth is actively held,
- execution is delayed.

A founder or explicitly designated authority may act as a **temporary truth-holder**.

This role is:
- rare,
- non-scalable,
- structurally necessary when infrastructure is incomplete.

Live Event State always exits back to normal operation.

---

## 6. Event as Asset

An Event becomes an asset **only after finalization**.

Its value does not arise from:
- tickets,
- hype,
- speculation.

Its value arises from:
- authorship,
- integrity,
- reference power,
- reuse without distortion.

Events do not behave like stocks.  
They accumulate leverage like foundational IP.

---

## 7. Truth vs. Value Capture

Truth must remain **unowned**.

Protocols, presence registries, and recognition layers may be:
- public,
- CC0,
- non-monetized.

Everything built **on top of truth** may be monetized.

This separation is mandatory to prevent corruption.

---

## 8. Founder Value Layer

Founder value must accumulate **above operations**, not inside them.

A founder-owned aggregation entity may:
- hold event IP,
- license formats,
- aggregate derivatives,
- route surplus,
- compound value.

It must not:
- own truth,
- decide presence,
- interfere with gates,
- corrupt governance.

Founder value is **containment of contribution**, not extraction.

---

## 9. Shells and Derivatives

NFTs, tokens, media, and currencies are **containers**, not sources.

They are:
- optional,
- replaceable,
- portable.

If a shell disappears, truth remains.  
If truth remains, value can re-emerge.

---

## 10. Interaction Law

This system rejects:
- narrative control,
- premature meaning allocation,
- authority by persuasion.

It requires:
- explicit state transitions,
- respect for cognitive bandwidth,
- non-compression of integrator cognition.
